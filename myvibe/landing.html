Common questions

Everything you need to know about vibe coding

What exactly is "vibe coding"?

Vibe coding means describing what you want in natural language, and AI generates deployable UI—not just designs. You describe, MyVibe deploys. No handoff to developers, no waiting for builds.

Do I need to know how to code?

Nope. That's the whole point. You describe your vision, MyVibe handles the technical implementation. Of course, if you're technical, you can dive into the output anytime.

What can I build with MyVibe?

Landing pages, portfolios, interactive demos, data dashboards, content hubs, and more. If it's a web interface, you can vibe it into existence.

How does A/B testing work?

Every change creates a version. Split traffic between variants with one click, compare performance, and promote winners—all without touching code or setting up infrastructure.

Is there a free tier?

Yes! Start free with 100 credits. Most users find that's enough to ship their first few vibes and experience the magic. See pricing

How does MyVibe compare to no-code builders?

No-code builders make you drag and drop. MyVibe lets you describe. You think in outcomes ("I want a hero with social proof"), not in components. It's the difference between giving directions and just saying the destination.
