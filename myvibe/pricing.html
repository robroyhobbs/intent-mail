Questions & Answers

What are credits?

Credits are the universal currency for AI operations in MyVibe. Every time the AI generates, iterates, or deploys something for you, it uses credits. Think of them like tokens that power the magic.

Do credits expire?

No! Credits you purchase never expire. Monthly credits from plans reset each billing cycle, but any purchased credits stay in your account forever.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes. You can change plans at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, downgrades at the end of your billing cycle. Unused monthly credits don't carry over.

What happens if I run out of credits?

Your published vibes stay live. You just won't be able to generate new content or make AI-powered changes until you add more credits or your monthly allowance resets.

Is there a free trial?

The Free plan is your trial—forever. You get 100 credits to start and can publish up to 3 vibes. No credit card required.

How do team credits work?

Team credits are shared across all team members. Any team member can use credits from the shared pool, and admins can see usage by member.
