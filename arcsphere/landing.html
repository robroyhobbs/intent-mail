Questions & answers

Everything you need to know about ArcSphere

What's an Agent Fleet?

An Agent Fleet is your personal collection of AI agents that work for you—research, writing, data analysis, and more. ArcSphere gives you mobile access to manage and interact with your fleet anywhere.

Is ArcSphere just a browser?

No. ArcSphere is your window into the AI-enabled internet. While it can browse, its real power is accessing AI services, skills, and your Agent Fleet—things regular browsers can't do.

What are AI Skills?

Skills are AI capabilities you can activate instantly—summarization, translation, code analysis, image generation, and hundreds more. Browse the skill library and tap to activate.

How does Spatial Tab Navigation work?

Your tabs are arranged on a 3D sphere. Spin to browse, tap to select. Find any tab in under 3 seconds, even with 50+ open. It's the end of tab chaos.

Is ArcSphere free to download?

Yes! Download free on iOS and Android. Basic features are free forever. Pro unlocks unlimited skills and fleet access. See pricing

What's the Action Ring?

A radial touch menu that appears when you hold. AI actions are arranged in a ring—spin to the one you want, release to execute. AI at your fingertips, literally.
