Questions & Answers

Is ArcSphere really free?

Yes! All core features—browsing, Skill Browser, Action Ring, Spatial Tabs—are completely free. You only need credits when using AI-powered features.

What are credits used for?

Credits power AI operations: chatting with AI, using Skills, and directing your Agent Fleet. Basic browsing and navigation don't use credits.

Do credits expire?

Purchased credits never expire. Monthly Pro credits reset each billing cycle, but any purchased credits remain in your account forever.

What's Agent Fleet access?

Agent Fleet is your personal crew of AI agents. Pro users get mobile access to monitor, direct, and interact with their agents from anywhere.

Can I upgrade anytime?

Yes. Upgrade to Pro anytime from within the app. Your credits and features activate immediately.

Is there a family plan?

Not yet, but it's on our roadmap. For now, each user needs their own account and credits.
