# ArcSphere

> Your real-time window into the AI-enabled internet

## TL;DR

ArcSphere is the **first mobile app with instant access to Agent Fleets and Skills**—a real-time window into the ever-growing AI-enabled internet. While other ArcBlock products focus on creation (MyVibe, DocSmith), ArcSphere is built for **consumption**: experiencing, exploring, and interacting with AI-powered content and services.

**What:** Radial touch menu for instant AI actions
**Why:** Transforms AI from chat to gesture-based commands

**Messaging:**

- "Tap, spin, select—done"
- "AI moves at the speed of touch"

**What:** Create and use custom AI profiles
**Why:** Users become AI creators, not just consumers

**Messaging:**
- "Build your own AI crew"
- "Your AI, your rules"
- "Custom skills for your workflow"

### 5. Spatial Tab Navigation (Spheres)
**What:** 8×8 grid rendered as 3D sphere for visual management
**Why:** Ends "tab chaos"—organize your AI-enabled browsing

**Messaging:**
- "Find any tab in 3 seconds"
- "End tab chaos instantly"
- "50+ tabs? No problem."

### 6. AI Chat (Context-Aware)
**What:** AI conversation that understands your browsing context
**Capabilities:**
- @ mention tabs as context
- Chat across multiple tabs
- Reference marked notes
- Switch AI models
- Voice input

### 7. Digital Passport (DID)
**What:** Passwordless identity authentication
**Why:** Seamless login across the ArcBlock ecosystem

**User-facing language:**
- "Your digital passport"
- "Login with a tap"
- "One identity, everywhere"

### 8. Content Consumption
**What:** Native rendering of MyVibe content, DocSmith docs, agent outputs
**Why:** ArcSphere is where you experience what the ecosystem creates

## Working With This Context

### When building features:
- Ask: "Does this improve consumption of AI content?"
- Ask: "Does this make Fleet access better?"
- Design for thumb-first mobile interaction
- Prioritize real-time, instant access

### When writing copy:
- Lead with consumption and access
- Frame as "window into AI internet"
- Emphasize Fleet access (first mobile app)
- Avoid creation language (that's MyVibe/DocSmith)

### When making product decisions:
- Consumption > Creation
- Fleet integration is strategic priority
- Skills should be instantly accessible
- Mobile-first, always
